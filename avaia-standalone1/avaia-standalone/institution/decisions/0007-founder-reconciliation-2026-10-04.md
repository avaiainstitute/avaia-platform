# Decision 0007 — Founder reconciliation of 2026-10-04

**Status:** Recorded, governing. These are the OWNER's decisions, made after the four-system
audit (Room/Table, Preparation GPT, Virtue Formula, Virtue Signature). Where a document or an
implementation disagrees with this file, this file wins until the owner says otherwise.

## Closed

1. **Room / Table ownership.** The Host always owns the Room and the Table. The Host's
   experience establishes the Room. The Guide facilitates within the Host's Room and never
   owns it.
2. **Witness.** The Witness is not a person. It is a standing function and presence at the
   Table whose purpose is to ensure that every relevant voice can have a voice and that no
   single voice takes over. The voices may be people, virtue elements or families, fear,
   doubt, guilt, shame, a younger or future self, Secondary Losses, Empty Chair
   perspectives, and other invited perspectives. It safeguards visibility and balanced
   participation; it does not control the outcome and does not become another participant.
   "Grief requires a witness."
3. **Preparation GPT.** A human Guide-side tool used while the Guide is actively helping the
   Host. It helps the human Guide translate what is happening into useful activities and
   practical applications, while preserving the Host's meaning, capacity and ownership and
   the Guide's boundaries. It is not primarily Host self-directed preparation,
   pre-conversation preparation, or between-session preparation.
4. **Virtue Formula outcome.** The governing term is **Desired Outcome**, not "Observable
   Outcome." The direction is: what is my desired outcome, and what virtue elements already
   within me can I draw upon, within my capacity, toward that outcome. The person owns the
   Desired Outcome.
5. **Virtue Signature, repeated pattern.** A Virtue Signature becomes increasingly visible
   through repeated experiences, repeated expressions, and different scenarios. It is not a
   list of virtues chosen once. No scoring, frequency threshold, classification or algorithm
   exists or is to be invented for deciding when repetition is "enough."
6. **Virtue Signature structure.** Center: YOU / IDENTITY. First ring: Vulnerability +
   Authenticity. Next ring: may hold up to eight virtue elements. Additional rings: may
   allow the Signature to keep becoming visible. Capacities for later rings are NOT
   established (the historical transcript's 2 / 8 / up to 18 are atom facts, not a rule for
   the Signature).
7. **The six Signature categories are not architecture.** "What I Recognize in Myself,"
   "What Other People Have Noticed," "How My Qualities Work Together," "Different Ways the
   Same Quality Can Show Up," "What I Want to Practice," "How I Want to Contribute." They
   first appear in AI/script-generated documents saved 26–27 August 2026
   (`AVAIA_My_Virtue_Signature_Youth_Experience_Blueprint.docx` and
   `AVAIA_My_Virtue_Signature_Master_Format_Kit.docx`, authored by `python-docx`, no earlier
   Founder source, no reason given for six, the Kit itself later uses eight take-home rows,
   nothing connects them to the atom). The source documents are kept as history, not deleted.
8. **DORIAN.** D — Dignity, O — Originality, R — Respect, I — Individuality (the transcript
   also says "Intuition"), A — Authenticity, N — **Nobility** (not "Ability").

# Decision 0006 — Keep this: Host ownership and continuity

**Status:** Recorded, governing. Implemented in Move 7 (migration `0117`).

## The rule

Something created or recognized through an AVAIA experience becomes part of a Host's
continuing record only when the **Host intentionally chooses to keep it**.

```
Host experiences / creates something
  -> Host decides what to keep
  -> the kept item is a Host-owned record
  -> it appears in the Host's Workbook
  -> the Host controls it from then on
```

## What this means in practice

- **A Guide may offer something back to the Host for consideration. A Guide may never decide
  that something belongs in the Host's record.**
- For anything that came through a Guide-run session, the Host must (1) confirm the session
  is theirs, (2) see the item, and (3) choose Keep this. Until the Host confirms, an offer shows
  only metadata: who offered it, when, and what kind of item. Only that Host action creates the
  Host-owned kept item.
- A Guide-run session happening through a Guide's account does not make the Guide the owner of
  the Host's story. A kept item records the Guide only as provenance, where it came from.
- Private processing is not automatic preservation. Nothing from IAP, CAT, InnerCompass,
  Workbook conversations or any other private Host content is kept because a system or an AI
  thinks it matters. Only Host-authored items (the Host's own words and chosen direction) and
  the Host's recognitions can be offered or kept, never a stage's own synthesis, and never a
  whole conversation.
- The Guide is never told what the Host decided. A Guide sees an offer only while it is
  waiting, and can withdraw it until the Host answers.
- The Host can take a kept item back out of their record (and put it back). What was kept is
  never rewritten.
- A person who creates an account after a session can claim the connection through a
  verified email that matches the one the Guide recorded; an email that is not verified, or a
  participant linked to a different account, never matches.
- A Guide may never place a participant's material in a Virtue Signature or any other record the
  Guide controls. Offering is the only path.

## Enforcement (not only documentation)

The database enforces it: kept items have only the Host's own policies (no Guide, admin or
operational policy exists), a Host cannot forge a "came through a Guide" item from a browser,
and what was kept cannot be rewritten. A Guide's offer is a pointer and a label with no text; the
Guide can create and withdraw a waiting offer but has no update policy. A Guide has no write
policy on a participant's Virtue Signature. The System Checks prove all of this against the live
catalog and with simulated records.

## Not part of this decision

Estate, legacy, beneficiaries, inheritance, incapacity, post-death access, a major export
system, and any redesign of the Workbook. Rooms are untouched. Youth participants receive no
offers while Youth Guide facilitation remains on hold.

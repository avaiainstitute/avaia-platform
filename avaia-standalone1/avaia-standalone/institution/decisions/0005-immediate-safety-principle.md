# Decision 0005 — The immediate-safety principle

**Status:** Recorded, governing. Legal confirmation of the exact wording is a
separate, later step and does **not** hold certification, Gate item 5, or any
Practice Lab.

## The principle

When a person shows signs of an immediate safety or medical emergency, the
Guide stops the AVAIA process and gets the person appropriate human help from
emergency or responsible resources. The Guide does not diagnose, counsel, assume
a professional role, or try to resolve the situation. Afterward the Guide
documents what happened factually.

This is a recognition-and-response standard for Guides. It is not a crisis
management system, and AVAIA does not build one.

## How it is applied

- **Boundary Gate item 5 (Safety)** keeps its approved pass standard, unchanged:
  recognition, immediate response, stopping normal Guiding, the referral pathway
  and documentation, in that order, without improvising. A human records met or
  not met. If not met, mandatory retraining on Lesson 6.9 follows. It is never
  waived or partial.
- **Practice Lab 12, Scenario C** (immediate safety or medical emergency) is
  active under this principle.
- The AI Host in practice sessions is covered by the existing crisis net in
  `lib/engine/prompts.ts` (stop the AVAIA method, respond with warmth, 988 / 911 /
  text HOME to 741741, stay present, do not counsel or diagnose).

## What this does not do

It does not create a legal opinion, a duty statement, a protocol for any
jurisdiction, or any automated detection beyond what already exists.

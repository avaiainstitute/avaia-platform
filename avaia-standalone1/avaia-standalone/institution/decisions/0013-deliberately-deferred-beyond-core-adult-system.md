# Decision 0013 — Deliberately deferred beyond the core adult system

**Status:** Recorded, governing. Founder decision, 2026-10-10. Nothing was built or changed in the product for this decision.

## The rule

The following are **DEFERRED**. They are not unfinished, not defects, and not blockers. They are not required to finish the core adult
system, and the certification readiness work explicitly separated them from first-release requirements.

1. Public Find-a-Guide marketplace
2. Guide booking / scheduling marketplace
3. Full Faculty architecture
4. Guide Trainer authorization
5. Complex Event Lead authorization
6. International certification structure
7. Long-term appeals board
8. Organization-specific certification variants
9. Youth Guide endorsement (distinct from the Youth Guide facilitation specialty, which is held separately)

## What this means in practice

* Audits, System Checks, What Needs Dorian and closure reports list these as DEFERRED, never as failures or "needs build".
* The public Certified Guide page already states that certification grants no Complex Event Lead, Guide Trainer, or Faculty authority.
  That statement is accurate and stays.
* Do not build, design, or imply any of them on the public site until a later explicit Founder decision.

## What would change this

Only a later explicit Founder decision naming the item.

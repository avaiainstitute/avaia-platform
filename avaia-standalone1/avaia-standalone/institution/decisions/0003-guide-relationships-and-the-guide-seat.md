# Decision 0003 — Guide relationships and stepping out of the Guide seat

**Status:** Recorded, governing. These are the OWNER's stated principles. They
are NOT the lesson. Certification lesson 6.12 ("Dual Relationships & Conflicts
of Interest") remains HELD and unwritten until the owner approves its final
instructional content. Nothing in this file is to be paraphrased into lesson
text, Companion answers, or any candidate-facing screen on anyone's own
authority.

## The principles (as stated by the owner)

- No relationship category is automatically prohibited solely because of the
  relationship.
- AVAIA may strongly discourage Guide relationships where an existing
  personal, family, romantic, employment, authority, dependency, emotional, or
  historical relationship may interfere with appropriate Guide-seat
  boundaries.
- Either the Host/participant OR the Guide may choose to change or end the
  Guide relationship.
- No explanation or justification is required.
- A Host may simply say they need another Guide, want to continue
  independently, or need to proceed differently.
- A Guide may simply say they need to step out of the Guide seat.
- AVAIA must not require disclosure of the underlying reason as a condition of
  making that change.

## What this does and does not settle

Settles: the posture a future lesson 6.12 must be consistent with.

Does not settle: the lesson's wording, any list of discouraged relationship
types presented to candidates, any Guide-transition or Host-continuity
mechanics (lesson 7.13, still HELD, access/continuity policy unresolved), or
mandatory-reporting and legal obligations (lesson 6.16, still HELD, legal
review required).

## How an approved lesson would be published

The classroom reads the curriculum from `lib/certification-content-data/
content.json`. A HELD lesson has `tag: "HELD"` and empty fields. Publishing an
owner-approved lesson means replacing that entry with the approved content and
clearing the tag; no code change is needed, and until then the classroom shows
the lesson honestly as pending and refuses to mark it complete.

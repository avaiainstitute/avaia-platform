# Decision 0001 — Green / Yellow / Red is a capacity signal

**Status:** Recorded, governing. Not yet implemented. Governs the Mobile V2
"Private Green/Yellow/Red Status" and "Status Sharing" work audited in the
AVAIA Mobile Version 2 audit, when that work is separately approved and begun.

## The decision

AVAIA's Green/Yellow/Red status system is fundamentally a **capacity
signal** — not, by default, an emergency or crisis classification system.

The traffic-light metaphor matters: a red light means *stop*, not panic,
danger, emergency, or that something catastrophic has occurred.

- **GREEN** — "I have capacity." The person is doing okay and currently has
  capacity for what they are carrying.
- **YELLOW** — "My capacity is getting stretched." Something is becoming
  difficult. The person may need understanding, connection, space,
  practical help, or simply someone to notice.
- **RED** — "I'm at capacity." The person needs something to stop, slow
  down, change, or be carried with them. RED is the proverbial bell: "I
  hurt." "I need something." "I could use some help." "I may not know how
  to ask." The person does **not** have to know what they need before
  selecting Red.

A Red status may optionally include a Host-authored need/request, for
example: *come sit with me; call me; check in with me; take a walk with
me; help me make a decision; bring me some water; help with the kids
while I rest; invite me somewhere; give me some space but check again
later; I don't know what I need, please ask.* These are examples, **not**
a fixed taxonomy — the Host owns the meaning and the request.

## Binding rules for any future implementation

1. AVAIA/AI must **never** infer that someone is Green, Yellow, or Red
   from private conversation content.
2. AVAIA/AI must **never** automatically change the person's status.
3. AVAIA must **never** reinterpret Red as a diagnosis, psychological
   assessment, or automatic crisis determination.
4. Status is intentionally **selected by the person**, always.
5. For Trusted Circle sharing, the shared information must remain narrow:
   the selected status, the Host-authored meaning/request (if any), and
   appropriate timestamp/status metadata — nothing else. *"Signal shared;
   story remains owned by the person."*
6. Sharing a status must **never** expose private Journey, IAP, CAT,
   InnerCompass, Workbook, Secondary Loss, Chemistry of Virtue, AI
   interpretation, or any other private AVAIA content.

## Explicitly out of scope for this decision

This decision does **not** establish crisis-response, mandatory-reporting,
Youth, legal-duty, or emergency-escalation policy. Those remain separate
policy/legal matters, to be addressed on their own when raised — not
inferred from this decision.

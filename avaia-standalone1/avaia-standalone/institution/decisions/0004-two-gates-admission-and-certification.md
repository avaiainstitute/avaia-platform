# Decision 0004 — Two gates: admission makes a Candidate, certification makes a Guide

**Status:** Recorded, governing. Amends the payment timing in Decision 0002
(the sequence and the human admission decision in 0002 stand; only *when the
$1,495 is charged* changes). Governs the Certification front door, candidate
access, the admin workflow, candidate-facing language and the public Certified
Guide page.

## The two gates

There are two separate human decisions, and neither implies the other.

```
GATE 1  Admission decision        ->  Candidate
GATE 2  Certification decision    ->  AVAIA Certified Guide
```

- **Admission is not a promise of certification.** Being admitted makes a person
  a Certification Candidate: it gives them candidacy, the AVAIA access they need
  to complete the certification path, and the chance to be evaluated. Nothing
  more. Whether a Candidate becomes a Certified Guide is decided later, by a
  human, from recorded evidence.
- **Certification is a human decision.** The system adds up what humans recorded.
  AI may support practice. AI never evaluates, scores or certifies.
- Candidate-facing language, the admin workflow, the public Certified Guide page
  and every governing document must keep these two gates visibly distinct.
  Never write that admission "leads to" or "results in" certification.

## Application and admission

1. The person applies while signed in: name and contact, why they are
   interested, work or community context, how they imagine using AVAIA, whether
   this is core certification or another permission, and agreement to receive
   orientation (Prospectus v0.1). No application fee. No invented admission
   criteria.
2. The person provides a payment method and authorizes it. **Nothing is charged.**
3. A human reviews the application.
4. A human records the admission decision.
5. **Only if admitted** is the $1,495 charged to the saved method, once.
   A person who is not admitted is never charged, and the saved method is removed.
6. Acceptance creates the candidacy and the access. Access begins at
   acceptance, not after payment.

If the charge fails, the admission stands. The failure surfaces for the owner
with a retry. A failed charge never revokes an admission.

Denial messaging, reapplication, and any refund policy are not decided and are
not invented (see Decision 0002: `POLICY_REQUIRED`).

## Candidate access

Candidacy provides the AVAIA access needed to complete certification. That
access is tied to candidacy. It is not a permanent free membership, and it ends
when candidacy ends (same rule as classroom access). A Candidate who already has
a membership keeps it untouched.

## Same standard for everyone

Individual and organizational candidates meet the same certification standard.

## The locked certification backbone

1. required classroom education;
2. personal Host-seat experience;
3. the Boundary Gate;
4. practice before independent Guide work (all 15 approved Practice Labs);
5. the Table-building exercise (its own exercise);
6. the Observed Practicum;
7. the human certification decision.

Held lessons 6.12, 6.16 and 7.13 stay held and are not certification blockers.
Toolkit/experience assembly is a specialty authorization, not a core
certification requirement.

# Conversations Across Time (CAT)

## Purpose

Increase understanding by exploring relationships, perspectives,
meanings, and patterns.

## Position

IAP → CAT → InnerCompass

## Primary Objectives

-   Increase visibility
-   Deepen understanding
-   Clarify tensions
-   Prepare referral

## Inputs

-   IAP referral
-   Guided conversation

## Outputs

-   Understanding summary
-   Referral to InnerCompass

## Boundaries

-   No directing decisions
-   No outcome control

# Chemistry of Virtue

## Purpose

Provides the framework for understanding how the AVAIA virtues work
together to support understanding, restoration, character development,
and intentional participation.

## Note

This implementation uses the AVAIA Chemistry of Virtue as defined by the
institution. Definitions, formulas, symbols, relationships, and
applications shall come from the official AVAIA source materials.

## Responsibilities

-   Organize virtue relationships
-   Support virtue formulas
-   Integrate with conversations
-   Support report generation

## Boundaries

-   Do not invent virtue definitions
-   Do not alter institutional content

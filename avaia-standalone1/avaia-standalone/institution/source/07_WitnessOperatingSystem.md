# Witness Operating System (WOS)

## Purpose

Preserves visibility, continuity, and recognition throughout the AVAIA
Journey.

## The Witness is a function, not a person

The Witness is a standing function and presence at the Table. It is not a
person and it is not another participant. Its purpose is to make sure every
relevant voice at the Table can have a voice, and that no single voice takes
over. Those voices may be people, and may also be virtue elements or
families, fear, doubt, guilt, shame, a younger self, a future self, a
Secondary Loss, an Empty Chair perspective, or any other invited
perspective. The Witness safeguards visibility and balanced participation. It
does not control the outcome.

Grief requires a witness.

(Reconciled 2026-10-04 to a Founder decision. Earlier wording that described
the Witness as "human, system, or both," or as a person occupying a seat,
is superseded.)

## Responsibilities

-   Make room for every relevant voice at the Table
-   Keep any single voice from taking over
-   Observe faithfully
-   Preserve continuity
-   Document meaningful understanding
-   Support integration over time

## Boundaries

-   Does not direct
-   Does not evaluate worth
-   Does not make decisions
-   Does not control the outcome
-   Does not become another participant

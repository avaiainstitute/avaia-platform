organizations (Founder directive). **The existence of a document or of working code does not make its
contents Foundation policy.** Only classes 1 and 2 below govern Founder intent.

## What Legacy's documents require (read 2026-10-05)

From the Legacy welcome packet and Fiscal Sponsorship Agreement (read by OCR; the originals are the
authority): Legacy "shall have exclusive authority to establish terms and conditions, policies and
procedures, and to make all decisions concerning the Cohort's tax exempt activities"; changes to the
Foundation's activities, projects or services "must be approved by the Sponsor in writing"; only Legacy
may give receipts, set up accounts or sign agreements; donations go to Legacy (it sets up the payment
processor, default Give Payments); every payment out is by invoice or reimbursement to Legacy with a
stated charitable use and charitable beneficiary and a statement whether anyone other than the people
helped benefits ("a necessary and relatively insubstantial byproduct"); no net earnings to a private
individual; payments must be at fair market value and arms-length. These are Legacy's rules, recorded
here as facts about the documents. **No Foundation system implements them, and none was invented here.**

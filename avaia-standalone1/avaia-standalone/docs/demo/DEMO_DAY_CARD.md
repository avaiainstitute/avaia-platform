2. **Check.** Every row on that page must say **PASS** and the heading must say "Ready: every check passes." (The "Check the demo" button re-checks without changing anything.)
3. **Two browser profiles, signed in once each:**
   - **Window A, Eleanor (the Host):** open `https://demo.avaiainstitute.com/sign-in?from=/workbook`, sign in as `kidathart+avaia-demo-host@gmail.com`. You land on her Workbook.
   - **Window B, Nora (the Guide):** open `https://demo.avaiainstitute.com/sign-in?from=/guided-coordination`, sign in as `kidathart+avaia-demo-guide@gmail.com`. You land on Guided Coordination (it will say no hosts yet).
4. **Your inbox** (kidathart@gmail.com) open in a third tab. The three demo addresses deliver there.
5. Do NOT open /journey or any admin page.

## The 14 minutes
Follow `DEMO_PRESENTER_SCRIPT.md` row by row (Workbook, Coordination, the decision's timeline, Share With, the handoff link, Guide access, Nora's note, Eleanor's Guide lane, end access).

## If something goes wrong
- **Email not in your inbox:** look in Spam, click "Not spam". Or skip it: open the share from Eleanor's item page under "What was shared (the frozen copy, exactly as sent)".
- **A page shows the wrong account:** check the menu. **CERTIFICATION** in the menu means you are Nora. Sign out and use the bookmark.
- **Anything looks off:** run the reset and the check again. It takes two minutes and returns the demo to the exact baseline.

## Afterwards
